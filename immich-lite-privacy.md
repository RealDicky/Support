# Privacy Policy for Immich Lite

Last updated: October 20, 2026

**1. No Data Collection**
We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, photos, or usage data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

**2. You Own Your Server**
Immich Lite is a viewer for your **own** Immich server. The app connects directly to the server address you enter. Your login credentials, access token, and photos travel only between your device and your own server. The token is stored in your device's secure storage; it is never sent anywhere else.

**3. Manual Upload Only**
Photos and videos are uploaded only when you explicitly select them. The app never scans your photo library in the background and never uploads anything automatically.

**4. App Updates**
When configured, the app checks GitHub Releases for updates. This request goes to GitHub, not to us, and carries no personal data.
